Who We Are

A Diversified Mining and Resource Services Company

Shilungwa Mining and Resources (SMR) delivers integrated solutions across mining, construction, and energy sectors. We combine technical expertise, modern equipment, and a skilled workforce to execute projects efficiently, safely, and sustainably.

Founded by Nkosinathi Mthembi, a results-driven construction and mining professional, SMR is positioned to deliver innovative and reliable solutions with strong leadership and a commitment to safety, quality, and sustainability.

What We Do

Comprehensive Mining and Resource Services

From open cast mining and exploration through to hauling, processing, and rehabilitation, we deliver end-to-end solutions across the full mining value chain.

  • Open Cast Mining and Mining Consultancy
  • Hauling, Logistics and Mining Equipment Solutions
  • Material Wash Plants, Processing and Refinement
  • Exploration, Geotechnical and Dredging Services
  • Rehabilitation of Mines and Borrow Pits
