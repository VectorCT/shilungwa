Roles We Recruit For

There are no advertised vacancies at the moment. We recruit as projects are awarded, so we keep CVs on file for the roles below and contact suitable candidates when positions open.

Plant and Equipment Operators

Excavator, ADT, dozer, grader, loader and dredge operators with valid certificates of competence.

📂 Operations⏰ Project-based

Drivers

Code EC / C1 drivers with PrDP for haulage and site logistics.

📂 Logistics⏰ Project-based

Artisans and Diesel Mechanics

Qualified artisans to maintain and repair yellow-metal fleets, plant and wash-plant equipment.

📂 Maintenance⏰ Full-time / contract

Engineering and Technical

Mining and civil engineers, surveyors, geologists and geotechnical technicians.

📂 Engineering⏰ Full-time / contract

Safety, Health and Environment

SHE officers and environmental practitioners for site compliance and rehabilitation work.

📂 SHE⏰ Project-based

Supervisors and Foremen

Experienced site supervisors to lead crews on mining, civil and rehabilitation projects.

📂 Operations⏰ Full-time / contract
