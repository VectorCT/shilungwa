SMR Achieves 500 Days Without Lost-Time Injury

Shilungwa Mining and Resources has surpassed a landmark safety milestone, recording 500 consecutive days without a lost-time injury across all of its operational sites. The achievement is a testament to the company's unwavering commitment to a zero-harm culture and the effectiveness of its comprehensive safety management systems, which prioritise the wellbeing of every person on site.

Central to this record is SMR's investment in continuous safety training. Every employee and contractor undergoes rigorous induction programmes before setting foot on an active site, covering hazard identification, emergency response procedures, and the correct use of personal protective equipment. Refresher training sessions are conducted monthly, and specialised courses are provided for high-risk activities such as blasting, heavy equipment operation, and working at height in open cast mining environments.

Behavioural observation has become a cornerstone of SMR's safety strategy. Supervisors and safety officers conduct daily walkabouts, engaging directly with crews to identify at-risk behaviours before they lead to incidents. A confidential reporting system encourages workers to flag potential hazards without fear of reprisal, and every report is investigated and addressed within 48 hours. This proactive approach has shifted the safety culture from reactive compliance to genuine ownership at every level of the organisation.

Equipment maintenance plays an equally critical role. SMR operates a fleet of heavy machinery across its open cast mining, hauling, and material processing operations, and each unit follows a strict preventive maintenance schedule. Real-time condition monitoring systems alert maintenance teams to potential mechanical issues before they escalate, reducing the risk of equipment-related incidents and unplanned downtime. The company's workshops are staffed by qualified technicians who ensure that every vehicle and machine meets or exceeds manufacturer safety specifications.

Reaching 500 days is a milestone the entire SMR team takes pride in, but leadership has been clear that the goal is not a number — it is a mindset. Safety briefings continue to open every shift, incident simulations are run quarterly, and the company benchmarks its performance against international best practice standards. As SMR expands its operations and takes on new projects, maintaining this safety record remains a non-negotiable priority embedded in every contract, every procedure, and every decision.
