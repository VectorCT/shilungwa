SMR Expands Limpopo Operations With New Processing Facility

Shilungwa Mining and Resources has officially broken ground on a new mineral processing facility at its flagship Limpopo operations site. The multi-million rand investment represents one of the most significant capital expenditure projects in the company's history and underscores SMR's commitment to scaling its extraction and processing capabilities to meet growing market demand.

The new facility will house a state-of-the-art material wash plant and advanced grading technology, enabling SMR to process a wider range of mineral feedstock with greater precision. Once operational, the plant is expected to increase overall processing capacity by 35%, allowing the company to handle higher volumes while maintaining the quality standards that clients have come to rely on. The grading and classification systems will support finer material separation, reducing waste and improving yield across multiple mineral categories.

Beyond the technical upgrades, the project is set to create approximately 85 new permanent positions and over 200 construction-phase jobs, providing a meaningful economic boost to surrounding communities. SMR has committed to prioritising local recruitment and skills development, partnering with regional training institutions to ensure that community members are equipped for the specialised roles the facility will require.

The processing plant's design incorporates water recycling systems and dust suppression technology, reflecting SMR's broader sustainability objectives. Processed water will be recirculated through the wash plant cycle, significantly reducing freshwater consumption compared to conventional operations. These measures align with the company's environmental management framework and its obligations under current mining and water use licences.

Construction is expected to be completed by the fourth quarter of 2027, with commissioning and ramp-up scheduled for early 2028. The expansion positions SMR to better serve its existing client base while opening capacity for new contracts in material processing, grading, and classification — core services that have driven the company's growth over the past decade.
