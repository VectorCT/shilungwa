Our Capabilities

End-to-End Mining and Resource Services

Open Cast Mining

We deliver safe, efficient open cast mining operations using modern equipment and experienced crews, managing all aspects from overburden removal through to ore extraction and stockpile management.

Mining Consultancy

Our advisory team provides strategic guidance on mine planning, feasibility studies, regulatory compliance, and operational improvement to help clients make informed decisions at every stage of the mining lifecycle.

Hauling and Logistics

We coordinate end-to-end hauling and logistics solutions — from mine-site material movement to long-distance road haulage — ensuring consistent delivery timelines and cost-effective supply chain performance.

Mining Equipment Solutions

We supply, maintain, and manage modern mining equipment fleets tailored to project requirements, ensuring maximum uptime and operational efficiency on site.

Dredging Services

Our dredging capabilities support material extraction from waterways, dams, and coastal zones using specialised equipment and environmentally responsible methods.

Material Wash Plants & Processing

We operate material wash plants that clean, separate, and prepare raw materials for downstream use, delivering consistent product quality and throughput.

Material Grading & Classification

Our grading and classification services ensure materials meet precise specifications through screening, sorting, and quality control processes tailored to client needs.

Refinement Services

We provide refinement solutions that upgrade extracted materials to market-ready standards, adding value through advanced processing and quality assurance.

Oil Exploration & Related Services

Our oil exploration division offers survey, drilling support, and site management services for upstream oil and gas projects, leveraging our mining expertise in resource extraction environments.

Exploration Services

Our exploration programmes combine geophysical surveys, geochemical analysis, and field mapping to identify and validate high-potential mineral deposits with precision and minimal environmental disruption.

Geotechnical Services

We deliver geotechnical investigations, slope stability analysis, and foundation assessments to support safe and effective mine design and construction projects.

Pollution Control Dam Construction

We design and construct pollution control dams that manage contaminated water runoff, protecting surrounding ecosystems and ensuring regulatory compliance on mining sites.

Rehabilitation of Mines & Borrow Pits

Our rehabilitation team restores mined land and borrow pits to productive use through regrading, topsoil replacement, revegetation, and ongoing environmental monitoring.
