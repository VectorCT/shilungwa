Environment

Environmental Stewardship

Two of our core services — pollution control dam construction and the rehabilitation of mines and borrow pits — exist to reduce mining's environmental footprint. We apply the same thinking to our own operations.

  • Separating clean and dirty water with properly designed pollution control dams
  • Progressive rehabilitation: reshaping, topsoiling and revegetating as work advances
  • Dust, noise and spill controls on active sites and haul roads
  • Working to the conditions of each site's environmental authorisations

Community

Community Development

Mining should benefit the communities around it. We aim to create opportunities that last beyond any single contract.

  • Prioritising local recruitment for site roles
  • Using local suppliers and service providers where they can meet the requirement
  • On-the-job training that builds transferable skills, from plant operation to safety

Safety

Health and Safety

Everyone who works with us should go home safe. Safety planning starts before we mobilise and is part of every shift.

  • Site-specific risk assessments and safe work procedures before work starts
  • Inductions, daily toolbox talks and the right to stop unsafe work
  • Competent, appropriately trained operators and well-maintained equipment

Governance

Governance and Compliance

We run the business ethically and transparently, and we expect the same of the people and companies we work with.

  • Compliance with applicable mining, health and safety, and environmental legislation
  • Clear, honest contracting and reporting to clients
  • Zero tolerance for bribery and corruption
