Typical Engagements

How We Support Your Project

Mining

Open Cast Contract Mining

Overburden stripping, load-and-haul, ore extraction and stockpile management, planned around your mine schedule and safety requirements.

Logistics

Hauling and Material Logistics

On-site material movement and road haulage of ore, aggregates and product, with equipment and operators supplied to suit the volume.

Processing

Wash Plants, Grading and Refinement

Setting up and operating wash plants, screening and classification to bring material to the specification your buyers require.

Exploration

Exploration and Geotechnical Studies

Field exploration, geotechnical investigation and dredging assessments to help you decide whether — and how — to develop a resource.

Civil

Pollution Control Dams

Construction of lined pollution control dams and associated stormwater works to separate clean and dirty water on mining sites.

Rehabilitation

Mine and Borrow Pit Rehabilitation

Backfilling, reshaping, topsoiling and revegetation to return disturbed land to a safe, stable and usable condition.
