About SMR

Our Story, Mission & Values

Shilungwa Mining and Resources is a diversified mining and resource services company delivering integrated solutions across mining, construction, and energy sectors with technical expertise, modern equipment, and a skilled workforce.

Who We Are

Company Overview

Our Mission

To extract and deliver high-value mineral resources through disciplined operations, innovative technology, and an unwavering commitment to safety, environmental stewardship, and community development.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and sustainable mining company in the region, setting the standard for responsible resource development and long-term shared value creation.

Who We Are

Shilungwa Mining and Resources (SMR) is a diversified mining and resource services company delivering integrated solutions across the mining, construction, and energy sectors. We combine technical expertise, modern equipment, and a skilled workforce to meet the demands of complex projects across southern Africa.

Our Founder

SMR was founded by Nkosinathi Mthembi, a results-driven construction and mining professional with extensive experience in project management, civil engineering, and mining operations. Under his leadership, the company has successfully delivered complex infrastructure and mining projects, building a reputation for reliability, quality, and operational excellence.
